# Employee Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the annual, sick, and parental leave entitlements for all
full-time employees of the company. It applies to every department and location
unless a local labour agreement provides a more favourable provision.

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement

Annual leave accrues monthly at the rate of 1.5 days per completed month of
service, capped at 25 days per calendar year. Entitlement is prorated for
employees who join mid-year.

### 2.1 Accrual by Service Length

| Service length | Annual entitlement |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 year | 15 days pro-rated |
| 1 to 3 years | 20 days |
| 3 to 7 years | 23 days |
| More than 7 years | 25 days |

Employees with less than one year of service receive 15 days pro-rated based on
completed months, in addition to their monthly accrual.

### 2.2 Carry Over

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following
calendar year and must be taken before 31 March. Any balance above 5 days is
forfeited.

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical
certificate is required for any absence exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on termination.

## 4. Parental Leave

Employees are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave must be
taken within 12 months of the birth or placement of the child. Employees on the
probation period of 6 months are eligible after confirmation.

## 5. Leave Approval Process

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working
days in advance. Requests of 5 or more consecutive working days require written
approval from the reporting manager and a countersignature from the Head of
Department.

## 6. Public Holidays

Public holidays are additional to annual leave and are published each calendar
year by People Operations. Where a public holiday falls on a weekend, the
following Monday is observed as a holiday.
